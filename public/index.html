How Your Friend Can Connect in 3 Steps

1

Install the APK

Download the APK onto your Android phone and tap to install. If prompted, tap "Allow from this source".

2

Enter Mac Details

Open LinkToMac on your phone. Enter the host and port shown in the Mac app or your Pinggy tunnel (e.g. ap.pinggy.link : 43210).

3

Grant & Mirror

Tap Connect Wi-Fi, enable the Accessibility service for remote touch, and watch the phone screen appear live on Mac!
